The Complete Coffee Types Guide

Volumes, cups, caffeine content, origins — explained accurately.

Most coffee drinks are built on espresso. What changes between them is extraction length, dilution, milk ratio, and serving size — not magic. Below you’ll find accurate, café-standard information on how much coffee you actually get, how it’s served, and how much caffeine it contains.

Assumptions: Arabica beans · 18–20 g coffee dose · standard café recipes. Caffeine varies by bean and roast — values shown are averages.

Espresso-Based Coffees (No Milk)

Ristretto — Italy

Volume: 15–20 ml · Cup: espresso cup (60 ml) · Caffeine: ~50–60 mg
A short-pulled espresso using less water. Sweeter, thicker, less bitter than a standard espresso.

Espresso (Single) — Italy

Volume: 25–30 ml · Cup: espresso cup (60–90 ml) · Caffeine: ~60–65 mg
The reference drink: concentrated coffee extracted under pressure with crema on top.

Doppio (Double Espresso) — Italy

Volume: 50–60 ml · Cup: small ceramic cup (120 ml) · Caffeine: ~120–130 mg
Two espresso shots. Very common as the default espresso serving in specialty cafés.

Lungo — Italy

Volume: 60–90 ml · Cup: small coffee cup (90–120 ml) · Caffeine: ~70–80 mg
Pulled longer than espresso. More bitter due to over-extraction.

Americano — Italy / USA

Volume: 120–180 ml · Cup: coffee cup (200 ml) · Caffeine: ~60–130 mg
Espresso diluted with hot water. Strength decreases, caffeine does not.

Milk-Based Coffee Drinks

Cappuccino — Italy

Volume: 150–180 ml · Cup: cappuccino cup (180 ml) · Caffeine: ~60–65 mg
Equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and thick foam.

Flat White — Australia / New Zealand

Volume: 160–180 ml · Cup: low ceramic cup · Caffeine: ~120–130 mg
Double espresso with silky microfoam and no dry foam.

Latte — Italy (modern café style)

Volume: 240–300 ml · Cup: tall glass or mug · Caffeine: 60–130 mg
Milk-forward drink with thin foam. Caffeine depends on shot count.

Cortado / Gibraltar — Spain

Volume: 90–110 ml · Cup: small glass · Caffeine: ~60–65 mg
Equal parts espresso and lightly steamed milk. No foam.

Piccolo Latte — Australia

Volume: 90–120 ml · Cup: small glass · Caffeine: ~50–60 mg
Ristretto with steamed milk. Smaller and sweeter than a latte.

Breve — USA

Volume: 180–240 ml · Cup: mug · Caffeine: 60–130 mg
Like a latte, but made with half-and-half instead of milk.

Café au Lait — France

Volume: 240–300 ml · Cup: large mug · Caffeine: ~90–120 mg
Brewed coffee (not espresso) mixed with hot milk.

Affogato — Italy

Volume: ~30 ml espresso + ice cream · Cup: dessert glass · Caffeine: ~60–65 mg
Espresso poured over vanilla ice cream — dessert with caffeine.

Cold Brew — USA

Volume: 240–300 ml · Cup: glass · Caffeine: ~150–200 mg
Cold-extracted over many hours. Smooth and high in caffeine.
